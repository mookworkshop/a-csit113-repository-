Ordering Process

  1. Choose your preferred drink from our menu.
  2. Choose your preferred size.
  3. Enter your full name and contact number.
  4. Enter the quantity you want to order.
  5. Submit the order form.

After Submitting Your Order

After submitting your order, wait for the café to contact you using the contact number you provided.

Once you have been contacted, your order will be ready for pick up.
